# 📊 Customer Behavior Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview
The **Customer Behavior Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze customer purchasing patterns, subscription status, sales performance, and revenue across different customer segments.

The dashboard provides meaningful business insights that help understand customer behavior, identify high-performing categories, and support data-driven decision making.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 📈 Total Number of Customers
- 💰 Average Purchase Amount
- ⭐ Average Customer Review Rating
- 👥 Customer Distribution by Subscription Status
- 💵 Revenue by Product Category
- 🛍️ Sales by Product Category
- 👤 Revenue by Age Group
- 📊 Sales by Age Group
- 🎯 Interactive Filters for:
  - Subscription Status
  - Gender
  - Product Category
  - Shipping Type

## 📈 Insights

- Around **73%** of customers are non-subscribers.
- Clothing generates the highest revenue and sales.
- Young Adults contribute the highest revenue.
- Customer purchasing behavior varies across product categories and age groups.
- Interactive filters enable dynamic business analysis.
